⚖️ Architectural Deep-Dive: LLaVA-1.5 vs. SpaceLLaVA

Comparing the visual instruction tuning foundations and architectural differences that yield the 1.58% gap on SpatialMQA.

LLaVA-1.5 (7B)

Large Language and Vision Assistant

Liu et al. (NeurIPS 2023)
  • ✓
    Vision Pretraining: Trained on standard LLaVA visual instruction tuning data (665k conversational pairs) with 2D bounding box coordinate tokens.
  • ✓
    Zero-Shot Performance: Achieves 29.28% on SpatialMQA, barely exceeding random guessing (28.50%). Suffers severely from lack of metric 3D depth understanding.
  • ✓
    LoRA Fine-Tuned Performance: Surges to 46.56% (+17.28%) after 10 epochs on 3,780 SpatialMQA samples.
  • ✗
    Key Vulnerability: Y-axis depth reasoning ($A_y = 29.64\%$) is essentially random guessing. Cannot distinguish "in front of" vs "behind" from 2D pixel cues.
SpaceLLaVA

SpatialVLM Enhanced Architecture

Chen et al. (CVPR 2024 / Remyx)
  • ★
    Vision Pretraining: Explicitly initialized from SpatialVLM, fine-tuned on ~2 billion spatial QA tokens covering quantitative physical distance (meters), height, and absolute 3D Cartesian coordinates.
  • ★
    Zero-Shot Performance: 31.32% on SpatialMQA (+2.04% over LLaVA-1.5).
  • ★
    LoRA Fine-Tuned Performance: Achieves 48.14%, representing the state-of-the-art open-source baseline.
  • ★
    Key Superiority: Y-axis depth reasoning jumps to 51.85% (vs 29.64% for LLaVA), proving that SpatialVLM pretraining is vital for depth perception.

🚨 Why Do Models Cap at ~48%? Failure Mode Diagnostics

Quantitative error analysis based on Section 5.5 of the ACL 2025 paper: 200 manually inspected failure cases.

43%
FRS: Perspective Substitution Failure
The model fails mental rotation when queried "If you are the cat in the image...". It defaults back to the camera view, flipping left/right and front/behind.
21%
IRSO: Entity Grounding Failure
Incorrect recognition or confusion of subjects and objects in crowded, complex COCO scenes without bounding boxes.
18%
LCR: Lack of 3D Commonsense
Violates basic physics, support relationships (gravity), or occluded line-of-sight reasoning between 3D surfaces.
11%
IILN: Text & Character Relations
Fine-grained spatial ordering between individual letters or numbers on street signs, shirts, and packaging.
🚀 Novel Improvement Blueprint: How We Can Beat 48.14%

Concrete, research-grade methodological interventions designed to systematically solve the FRS and depth bottlenecks.

1
Spatial-CoT: Decomposed Mental Rotation Prompting
Instead of direct single-token choice, force the model through a 3-step Chain-of-Thought: (1) Identify observer's orientation vector $\vec{v}_{obs}$, (2) Locate subject and object 3D bounding positions, (3) Perform coordinate transformation matrix rotation before picking the final option.
Target Impact: Resolves FRS (43% of errors) by breaking implicit rotation into explicit steps.
2
Metric Monocular Depth Injection (Depth-Anything-V2)
The single biggest flaw of LLaVA is Y-axis depth collapse ($A_y = 29.64\%$). We can pass monocular metric depth maps or surface normals computed via Depth-Anything-V2 as an auxiliary visual channel to provide explicit depth tokens.
Target Impact: Lifts LLaVA Y-axis from 29.6% to >55%, directly closing the 3D depth gap.
3
Explicit Observer Frame Normalization
For Q2/Q3 questions, programmatically crop or visually annotate the observer entity with a soft visual directional arrow or orientation mask before feedforward processing, giving the vision encoder an explicit frame of reference.
Target Impact: Directly mitigates first-person perspective confusion without retraining.
4
Next-Gen MLLM Architecture (Qwen2-VL-7B)
LLaVA-1.5 is constrained by fixed $336 \times 336$ resolution and naive 2-layer MLP projection. Upgrading our backbone to Qwen2-VL (Native Dynamic Resolution + Multimodal RoPE) naturally improves fine-grained spatial grounding and text recognition.
Target Impact: Eliminates IILN & IRSO errors (32% of total errors) via higher spatial resolution.
