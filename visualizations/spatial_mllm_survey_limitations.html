⚠️ The 4 Fatal Limitations of Existing MLLMs

Why simply fine-tuning LoRA or concatenating Depth Anything maps leaves models stuck below 50% accuracy:

🔄

1. The Frame of Reference (FRS) Dilemma

Responsible for 38.2% of Spatial Errors

Vision encoders (CLIP) natively project scenes onto the 2D sensor array. When questions require ego-centric perspective shifts ("If you were the giraffe..."), the model cannot execute the mental 3D rotation matrix $R$, defaulting lazily to the camera's point of view.

Fails on: LLaVA-1.5 (40.9% on Q2), Spatial-MLLM (NeurIPS 24), SpatialVLM (CVPR 24).
🧠

2. Semantic Shortcut Exploitation

False Confidence in Benchmarks

On datasets like SQA3D or ScanQA, MLLMs achieve deceptive scores by memorizing language priors (e.g., "sun is above", "floor is below", "larger means closer"). When tested on SpatialMQA without bounding boxes and with non-canonical perspectives, performance drops to coin-flip level.

Fails on: GPT-4V, Gemini 1.5 Pro, BLIP-2, IDEFICS.
📷

3. The Sensor Dependency Barrier

Laboratory vs In-the-Wild Realities

Many specialized 3D models (PointLLM, 3D-LLM, ScanQA) require dense 3D point clouds or calibrated RGB-D cameras (Matterport / LiDAR). In contrast, real-world web applications and SpatialMQA operate exclusively on uncalibrated, monocular 2D images.

Fails on: PointLLM (ECCV 24), 3D-LLM (NeurIPS 23), Grounded 3D-LLM.
⚡

4. Semantic Alignment Catastrophe

Catastrophic Forgetting

Directly injecting 3D position embeddings or early-fusing depth tokens into pre-trained 2D ViT patch tokens corrupts the pre-trained CLIP semantic alignment space, causing severe degradation on open-ended general visual reasoning tasks.

Fails on: LLaVA-3D (2024), early dual-encoder variants.

🚀 True Novelty & Research Opportunity

The Unclaimed Frontier: Explicit 3D Coordinate Frame Transformation (Tcam→obj)

Because prior 2024 papers (Spatial-MLLM, LLaVA-3D) proved that depth alone cannot resolve Frame of Reference Shifts, our proposed framework introduces a neuro-symbolic bridge: estimating the object's 3D pose vector, computing the analytical transformation matrix, and conditioning LLaVA on the true relative 3D coordinate frame.

Step 1

Metric Monocular Depth

Extract per-pixel metric depth $(X_{cam}, Y_{cam}, Z_{cam})$ using Depth Anything V2 directly from 2D in-the-wild images without special sensors.

Step 2

Object Heading & Pose Estimator

Extract the 3D heading orientation vector $\vec{h} = (\theta, \phi)$ of the reference subject to locate its intrinsic front-facing axis.

Step 3

Analytical 3D Transformation

Compute the rotation matrix $R_{cam \rightarrow obj}$ to rotate target coordinates from Camera Frame into Subject Frame: $P_{obj} = R \cdot (P_{target} - P_{subject})$.

Step 4

Spatial-CoT Conditioning

Inject the transformed relative coordinate vector $(\Delta x', \Delta y', \Delta z')$ into LLaVA's instruction prompt, turning 40% guessing into rigorous geometric deduction.
